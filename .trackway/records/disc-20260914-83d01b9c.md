# Discovery

Five AppInventoryScannerTests ran against the real /Applications because TempHomeTestCase's default `applications` root is the real directory; the engine inventoried 42 real apps (~7s of tree measuring per test) and assertions failed on real data. Fix pattern: route every test through an `inventory()` helper that always injects `applicationsOverride`; suite went 35.9s to 0.018s.
